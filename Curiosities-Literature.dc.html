Literature

Long series, read in order, usually late.

Mostly manga, mostly long series, mostly read in order and mostly late at night. Long-form serialised storytelling is its own discipline: keeping a thread coherent across hundreds of chapters is a harder problem than most single novels attempt.

Reading in three languages also means noticing how differently the same idea lands depending on which one it arrives in, which is roughly the same skill six countries taught me.
