Weightlifting

Snatch and clean & jerk, programmed year-round.

I started lifting because I wanted to be stronger. It turned into the clearest lesson I have in patience: nothing here can be rushed, and the programme only works if you run it for months.

Olympic lifting in particular is unforgiving about technique. You cannot muscle a bad snatch. The lift simply fails, in front of everyone, which is a useful thing to get comfortable with.
