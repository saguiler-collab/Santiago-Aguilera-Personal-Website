Music & Drums

A kit, a pan, a piano and a voice

Drums at the centre since I was eleven, and a growing circle of instruments around them.

Every school I joined had a room with a kit in it. That is how I met people. I started on drums in Panama at eleven and kept going through three countries, and the circle widened: the glockenspiel, the piano, the Trinidadian steel pan and, at Perkiomen, my own voice with the rock band. Almost everything on these pages comes from the process journals I kept in music class at ISPS between Grade 8 and Grade 10.

My Instrument and Me: “Sunday Bloody Sunday” by U2, four and a half pages of sheet music, a retake on the summative and my first lessons in tuning an acoustic kit. Then Instrumental Communities: teaching two classmates the beat of “As It Was” and drumming in the Beauty and the Beast pit. Glockenspiel for the school song, drums on “Waka Waka” at International Day, my first time on a tenor steel pan for visitors from Alaska, and five drum pieces plus piano in Legally Blonde. The school song on piano, an 8 out of 8 on “Upside Down”, 29 out of 30 on the theory test, the “Take on Me” e-Portfolio pushed toward 186 BPM, and Mamma Mia!: eleven pieces prepared, two on stage. The rock band, on drums and now on vocals as well.
I believe that this quarter is the start of my full dedication to drumming.
