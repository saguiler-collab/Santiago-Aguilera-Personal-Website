MYP e-Portfolio

Grade 10: an EDM genre study, and “Take on Me” on drums at 186 BPM.

The e-Portfolio is the music project that closes the IB Middle Years Programme, running from November 2024 to March 2025. Mine has two tasks, both written in Spanish: a study of electronic dance music, and the weeks I spent learning “Take on Me” on drums. Both are here to read, page by page.

01

The brief

The unit's statement of inquiry was “art imagines possible futures”. Task 1 chose a genre and two songs, and justified the one I would perform. Task 2 followed the weeks of preparing it. The portfolio is assessed on the four MYP arts criteria: knowing and understanding, developing skills, thinking creatively, and responding.

02

Task 1 · The genre study

I studied electronic dance music because it is where technology rewrote what music could be. I compared two songs from the 1980s that used new machines to imagine a future.

  • “Your Love”, Frankie KnucklesHouse, 4/4 at 118 BPM in E minor, built on the Roland TR-909 drum machine.
  • “Take on Me”, a-haSynth-pop at 169 BPM in G minor, built on the Juno-60, DX7 and Linn Drum.
  • How I compared themTempo, key, structure, texture, dynamics, the lyrics and the machines behind the sound, plus MIDI and where EDM went afterwards.
  • The verdict“Take on Me”: more innovations, more impact, and a tempo that, as a drummer, opened my eyes.
03

Task 2 · Learning it

My artistic intention was to play the piece in an agile, powerful way that gives the audience a rush toward the future. I split the learning into the beginning, the middle and the end, and wrote a strengths, weaknesses and next-steps entry for each.

  • Week 1Learned the opening by ear and used the Moeller technique on the hi-hat. Added an open hi-hat at the end of four bars so the intro would not sound flat.
  • Weeks 2 and 3Found a drum-cover video at my level, played it at ×0.7 to ×0.8, then used a metronome to climb back to the original tempo. Added the fills, the tom and the ride.
  • Weeks 4 and 5Raised the speed by 0.05 to 0.1 each lesson up to ×1.1, which is nearly 186 BPM. I practised on the school acoustic kit and an electric kit at home.
  • Week 6A rehearsal performance in front of physics and drama students.

What went wrong, on the record

The backing video failed at the start of the rehearsal performance, so I played the whole piece from memory. The ending was strong. The middle was not: my fills ran slow at 3:10, 3:20 and 3:30, and I had worn out before the bridge. I wrote all of that down, along with the plan: a clearer structure for where each fill goes, and an outfit chosen to say something about the future.

04

A class of two

Between Music I in Grade 9 and Music II in Grade 10 the class went from eighteen people to two. The other was a good friend and a fellow drummer. We shared one kit, traded notes when one of us missed a lesson, and played the school song and the Trinidad and Tobago national anthem together.
