SonoArte

Where it started, in pandemic Panama, and kept going from Trinidad.

SonoArte is a music school in Panama, and it is where I started playing drums. I began in the middle of the pandemic, and when we moved to Trinidad in 2021 I kept taking lessons with SonoArte online.

01

Before the kit

I did not start with drums. I played the violin at four or five and the piano at six or seven, and stayed a beginner at both. When I stopped I did not expect to go back to music. Drums, at eleven, were the thing that stuck.

  • ViolinAround age four or five.
  • PianoAround age six or seven.
  • DrumsBack to music at eleven, and it stayed.
02

Starting in Panama

I arrived in Panama in July 2019, at age ten. Drums began at SonoArte, and then the pandemic arrived and the world went online. Lessons did too.

03

Then Trinidad

When we moved to Port of Spain in August 2021 the lessons did not stop. I kept taking SonoArte online from Trinidad, so the kit stayed part of the week through a move that changed everything else about it. By Grade 8 I was drumming about four hours a week, between class, an after-school lesson on Mondays and Thursdays, and practice at home.

  • PanamaWhere it started, in person and then online.
  • TrinidadLessons online, from a different country.
  • Grade 8About four hours of drumming a week.
04

Two kits

I own an electric kit at home, so volume has never been a problem there: I can turn it down and practise at any hour. The school's acoustic kit is a different instrument. In Grade 8 I had to learn to hit more softly so my classmates could hear their own parts, and to tune the drums with a key without breaking anything.

05

Why it stuck

It became the thing I do to think. A new country takes a while to feel like yours, but a rhythm that you practised in the last one is still yours on the first day.
