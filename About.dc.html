About me

Five places, in order

I was born in Monterrey and have lived in five countries since. Every place added something — a language, an instrument, a way of reading a room. This page is that story in order, plus the symbol I drew for it.

Where it starts: family, Spanish, and the city I still measure other places against. A new school at altitude, and a second accent in the same language. Where I started playing drums, at SonoArte. It became the thing I do to think. Student Council, and Stronger Together — a student-led initiative supporting classmates through the return to in-person school. Health science, HOSA, Model UN and GVMUN, Investment Club, the Perkiomenite, varsity swimming and the rock band.
The years for each move. Send them and the rail fills in.

The drawing was traced as a true Archimedean spiral — even spacing between turns, one continuous stroke, the two marks mirrored at the same angle I first drew them. The wobble is gone; the character is not.

It reads as a life map: a line that keeps circling back and still moves outward. On this site it earns its keep — as you scroll, the coil unwinds into the rail on the left, and the two marks become the arrows that point at whatever section you are reading. On the research pages the same line splits into a double helix.
