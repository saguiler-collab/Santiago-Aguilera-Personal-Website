About me

Six countries, in order

I was born in Houston, Texas, in the middle of Hurricane Ike, and have lived across six countries since: Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Panama, and Trinidad & Tobago, before boarding at Perkiomen in Pennsylvania. Every place added something: a language, an instrument, a way of reading a room. This page is that story in order, plus the symbol I drew for it.

Born September 13, 2008, in the middle of Hurricane Ike, which is where the spiral on this site started, in more ways than one. Age two. The first move, though I don't remember it. It's the one that started the pattern. Age three. School started here, AIM then LICEO, and it's still the city I measure every other place against. Age eight, December 2016. A new school at altitude, at Colegio Tilata in La Calera, and a second accent in the same language. Age ten, July 2019. International School of Panama, and where I started playing drums, at SonoArte. It became the thing I do to think. Age twelve, August 2021. Student Council, and Stronger Together, a student-led initiative supporting classmates through the return to in-person school. Age sixteen, August 2025, as a boarding student. Health science, HOSA, Model UN and GVMUN, Investment Club, the Perkiomenite, varsity swimming and the rock band.

The drawing was traced as a true Archimedean spiral. Even spacing between turns, one continuous stroke, spinning outward the way a storm does. The two marks are mirrored at the same angle I first drew them: a gust caught mid-motion, not a static line.

It reads as a life map for exactly that reason: a shape that keeps circling back and still moves outward, the way I keep circling back to five countries by air. On this site it earns its keep. As you scroll, the coil unwinds into the rail on the left, and the two wind-marks become the arrows that point at whatever section you are reading. On the research pages the same line splits into a double helix.
