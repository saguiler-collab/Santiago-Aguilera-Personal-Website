Drums & Music

SonoArte Panamá, the ISPS musical, and the Perkiomen rock band.

I started at SonoArte in Panama, played the ISPS musical productions in Trinidad, and now play in the Perkiomen rock band. Three very different rooms, one kit.

That is not a small thing when you change countries every few years. Drumming is the reason arriving somewhere new has never meant arriving alone.
